HOW TO PLAY

Survive in the Frozen Tundra, the Scorched Dunes, the Whispering Woods or the Rocky Peaks with 3 other googlies (computer googlies or your friends). Keep your ❤️ health up: eat 🍗, drink 💧 at the lakes, and don't get too cold or too hot 🌡️. Nights are cold, desert days are roasting, and every day a storm rolls in.

Gather: walk up to a tree, rock, gold ore, berry bush, cactus or fishing hole and hold EUSE (or hold the mouse button). QEAT eats, RSIP sips from your canteen, EUSE at a lake drinks and fills the canteen.

Money: sell things at the Trading Post in the middle of the map (press EUSE at the counter). Prices change every day. Buy an axe, a pickaxe, a warm coat, a sun hat or a bigger backpack. Grab 📦 supply crates before anyone else does.

Shelter: go to your plot (the flag in your colour) and press BBUILD to build. There are 5 levels, from a lean-to to a fort or a palace. Inside your shelter you stay warm, heal fast and you're safe from storms.

Who wins? When the last day ends, the googly with the most money + shelter value wins (fainting costs you). FBONK bonks a googly so they drop something, but they can bonk you back!

On a phone or tablet: the stick on the left walks (push it all the way to run, or tap RUN). Drag anywhere on the right to look around, pinch to zoom. Hold USE to gather, drink, trade and open crates. BUILD at your plot, EAT, SIP, BONK, JUMP. Top right: 🏆 scores, 💬 chat, ☰ menu.
